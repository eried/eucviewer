Slow transfer notice

Five ways to say how long a Dropbox load or sync has left. Each one only appears once the estimate passes the threshold, counts down from a running average of the trips done so far, can be dismissed by clicking it, and leaves on its own when the transfer ends. Pick one.

chosen: none

How the estimate works: the first few trips are ignored while the rate settles, then remaining time is trips left x average seconds per trip, smoothed so the number does not jitter. The notice is only raised when that figure is over the threshold, so a quick transfer never shows one. Wording goes About 2 minutes left / About a minute left / Under a minute left.
